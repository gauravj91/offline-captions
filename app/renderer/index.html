Speech models & runtime

  • NVIDIA Parakeet-TDT 0.6B v2: English speech recognition. © NVIDIA, licensed CC-BY-4.0. Model card
  • AI4Bharat IndicConformer: Hindi word timing. MIT. Project
  • Oriserve Whisper-Hindi2Hinglish: Roman Hinglish text. Apache-2.0. Model card
  • OpenAI Whisper (via transformers.js / ONNX Runtime): other languages. MIT.
  • sherpa-onnx (k2-fsa): on-device inference. Apache-2.0. Project
  • FFmpeg + libass: video decoding and caption burn-in. LGPL-2.1.
