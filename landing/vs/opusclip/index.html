Offline Captions vs OpusClip

Short answer: OpusClip is a clipping tool: you give it a long video and its AI picks the moments, on monthly credits. Offline Captions doesn't pick clips. It captions the clips you already cut, on your own computer, with no credits, for $39 once. If you need the clip-finding, OpusClip does something we don't; if you already know your clips, you're paying for credits you don't need.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Offline Captions if
  • You cut your own clips (or film short-form directly).
  • You caption a lot of videos and don't want to watch a credit meter.
  • You want your dictionary of names and brand terms without a Pro plan.
  • Your footage shouldn't leave your laptop.
Pick OpusClip if
  • You want AI to find the best moments in long podcasts or streams.
  • You want a virality score and auto-reframing.
  • You schedule and publish to social accounts from one place.
  • You work in a shared cloud workspace with a team.

Use both, if clipping is your bottleneck

Plenty of creators cut clips in one tool and caption in another. If OpusClip's clip picks are what you value, the cheapest setup is often to find clips there and caption the exports in Offline Captions, so the caption style, dictionary and spelling stay identical across every clip without spending credits on re-renders.

FAQ

Is there an OpusClip alternative without credits?

For captions, yes. Offline Captions is a one-time $39 desktop app ($29 founding pre-order) with unlimited videos and no credits. It does not find clips in long videos the way OpusClip does.

How much does OpusClip cost in 2026?

OpusClip's pricing page lists Starter at $15 a month (monthly only) with 150 credits, and Pro at $29 a month or $14.50 a month billed annually ($174 a year) with 300 credits. The free plan has 60 credits, a watermark and exports that expire after 3 days (checked Oct 2026).

Can I caption OpusClip exports in Offline Captions?

Yes. Export your clips without captions, drop them into Offline Captions as a batch, and they all get the same animated style and dictionary.
