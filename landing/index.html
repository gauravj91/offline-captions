How it works

Three steps, a couple of minutes.

1

Drop in your videos

Drag one clip or a whole folder into the app. Everything stays on your computer.

2

Check the yellow words

The app transcribes locally and marks any word it isn't sure about in yellow. Fix those, skip the rest.

3

Pick a style and export

Apply your saved brand preset and export in 1080p or 4K, plus an .srt file if you need one.

FAQ

Questions

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription and rendering happen 100% on your computer. Your videos and audio never leave your machine, and the app works with no internet connection once it's installed.

Which computers does it run on?

Mac and Windows. We'll publish exact minimum specs before launch. Transcription runs on your own hardware, so newer machines finish faster.

How accurate is it?

Clear speech in a quiet room comes out very close to right. Names, slang and brand words are where any tool slips, which is why there's a personal dictionary and why unsure words are highlighted in yellow. You check those few words instead of proofreading the whole video.

What if it isn't for me?

You get a 30-day refund after launch, no questions asked. And the pre-order is refundable any time before launch: email us and we'll send your money back.
