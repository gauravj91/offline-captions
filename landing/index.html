How it works

Three steps, a couple of minutes.

1

Drop in your videos

One clip or a whole folder. Everything stays on your computer.

2

Check the yellow words

It transcribes locally and marks any word it isn't sure about in yellow. Fix those, skip the rest.

3

Pick a style and export

Bold, clean, punch or reveal. You get the captioned MP4 at your video's own resolution, plus an .srt file.

Personal dictionary

Correct a name or brand word once and it's remembered for every video after.

Filler-word removal

Drops "um" and "uh" from your captions with one switch.

Batch export

Caption a week of clips in one go. No credits to count.

Animated word highlighting

Each word is timed to the audio, so the highlight moves with your voice.

Hinglish mode

Hindi-English speech written in Roman script, the way your audience types it. More

Works offline

No account needed to caption, no upload queue, nothing to stop working when the Wi-Fi does.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is the best CapCut auto captions alternative that works offline?

If you want CapCut-style animated, word-by-word captions without a subscription or uploading your videos, Offline Captions is built for exactly that. It transcribes on your own Windows PC (Mac version coming soon), works with no internet connection, and costs $39 once ($29 at the founding price). CapCut Pro costs $19.99 a month or $179.99 a year and CapCut says its auto caption generator is exclusive to Pro (checked Oct 2026). CapCut is a full video editor, so if you need its effects and templates it can still be the better fit. Full comparison.

Is there a one-time-purchase caption app?

Yes. Offline Captions is a one-time purchase: $39 later, $29 for founding buyers. There is no subscription, no credits and no watermark, and there is no limit on how many videos you caption, because the work happens on your computer instead of a paid server.

How do I add Hinglish captions to a video?

Choose Hinglish mode, drop in your video and export. Offline Captions writes Hindi-English speech in Roman script, the way people type it (for example "Usko gaali de do, complaint kar lo"), with each word timed for the highlight animation. It runs offline on your computer. Words it could not time precisely are marked in yellow so you can check them. Hinglish details.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription and rendering happen on your computer. Your video and audio never leave your machine, and the app works offline once it and its speech model are installed.

How accurate are the captions?

On our own 28-clip benchmark of noisy, accented English, the English engine had a 7.6% word error rate, versus 9.6% for the widely used open alternative. We have not finished a head-to-head test against CapCut or Submagic, so we don't claim to beat them. Words the model is unsure of are highlighted in yellow and a personal dictionary remembers your names and brand words.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 today; the Mac version is coming soon. Transcription runs on your own hardware, so newer computers finish faster.

Can I get an SRT subtitle file as well as the video?

Yes. Every export gives you the captioned MP4 with the animation burned in, plus a standard .srt file for YouTube, LinkedIn or your video editor.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

No. YouTube's Terms of Service don't allow downloading videos outside YouTube's own features, so the app won't fetch YouTube links. Captions get burned in before you post, so you'll already have the original file, and it's better quality than a re-download too. If your own files are in the cloud, paste a Google Drive or Dropbox link in the desktop app. For a video that's already on YouTube, export an .srt file and upload it in YouTube Studio under Subtitles; there's no need to re-upload the video.

Can it caption a batch of videos at once?

Yes. Drop in a folder of clips, pick your style, and it captions them one after another while you do something else. There is no per-video charge.

Which languages does it support?

English is the main focus. Hinglish has its own mode with Roman-script output. Hindi in Devanagari and other Indian languages are coming; we'll say which ones when they're ready.

What if it isn't for me?

You're covered by a -day money-back guarantee. Email us and we'll refund you.

What is Shorts Studio, and can I buy it now?

Not yet. Shorts Studio is a planned add-on that turns a long video or podcast into ready Shorts on your own computer: it cuts silences and retakes, shortlists 8–15 clips for you to pick from, crops to vertical around whoever is speaking, and keeps your captions in sync. The planned price is $79 once with captions included, or a $40 upgrade for Offline Captions owners. It isn't for sale and nothing ships today.
