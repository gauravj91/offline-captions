FAQ

How do I caption podcast clips for Reels and Shorts?

Export the clips without captions, add them to Offline Captions as a batch, fix any yellow-flagged words (guest names go in the dictionary once), choose a style and export MP4s plus SRT files.

Do I need credits to caption podcast clips?

Not with Offline Captions. It is a one-time $39 purchase with no limit on clips or length.
