How we picked

We took the prompts creators type into AI assistants ("best one-time payment caption software", "which caption app lets you pay once and keep it forever", "caption tools without a monthly subscription") and collected the tools those answers cite, then added the offline desktop apps they tend to miss. A tool made the list only if its own site sells the captioning product for a single payment, with no subscription required to keep using it. We read each pricing page on 2 Nov 2026 and recorded price, device limits, where transcription runs, animation, exports and free-tier limits. Tools whose one-time price we could not confirm were left out. We have not benchmarked their accuracy against each other.

Who each is best for

  • Caption Plug: video editors who already work in Premiere Pro and want CapCut-style animated captions on the timeline, on up to 3 machines.
  • CapiCap: creators who shoot and post from an iPhone or iPad and want on-device captions for $9.99.
  • AI Subtitle Studio: people who need subtitle files (films, series, courses) with batch processing, not styled social captions.
  • WithSubtitles Desktop: short-form creators on Windows or Mac (Linux is experimental) who like the free browser editor and want batch export and offline work.
  • Subtitle Studio: editors who need clean SRT or native Final Cut Pro titles in 98+ languages.
  • Offline Captions: Windows creators who want animated word-by-word captions without opening an editor, with yellow flags on unsure words and a Hinglish mode.

The tools, one by one

Caption Plug

A Premiere Pro panel that generates animated, word-by-word captions on your timeline using a bundled local Whisper model, so it works offline; OpenAI or Groq keys are optional. $14.99 once covers 45 caption presets, 62 fonts, auto-censor, SRT export, 3 machines and all v1.x updates. You need Premiere Pro 2020 (v14) or newer, sold separately by Adobe. Its separate browser studio is pay-as-you-go after one free video.

CapiCap

Transcribes on the device and works in airplane mode once the speech model is downloaded. Twelve looks, word highlighting, burned-in or SRT export at original quality. The first 3 exports a month are free with no watermark; lifetime access is $9.99.

AI Subtitle Studio

A Windows and Apple-silicon Mac app for $9.99 once on up to 3 devices: offline transcription in 90+ languages, subtitle sync, batch processing and format conversion. Translation runs in the cloud on your own DeepSeek key or paid credits. It makes subtitle files; animated burned-in captions are not part of its pitch.

WithSubtitles Desktop

$49 once, with updates included. It adds up to 25x faster export, batch, saved styles, local fonts, SRT import and a command line to the free browser editor, and works offline. The maker says transcription is the same as the free version.

Subtitle Studio

$69 lifetime for one Mac or Windows device. Offline Whisper in 98+ languages, burn-in to MP4, bulk conversion, SRT and native Final Cut titles. Transcription and editing are free to try; export needs the licence.

Offline Captions (ours)

Windows now, Mac coming (no date yet). Word-by-word animated captions made on your computer, exported as an MP4 with captions burned in plus an SRT. English scored 7.6% word error rate on our 28-clip benchmark; Hinglish mode writes Hindi-English speech in Roman script. $39 once or $29 founding. The free browser version handles clips up to 5 minutes with no signup. Skip it if you edit in Premiere (Caption Plug fits better) or need translation.

FAQ

Which caption app lets you pay once and keep it forever?

Caption Plug ($14.99, Premiere Pro plugin), CapiCap ($9.99 lifetime), AI Subtitle Studio ($9.99), WithSubtitles Desktop ($49), Subtitle Studio ($69) and Offline Captions ($39, $29 founding) are all sold as one-time purchases.

What is the cheapest caption software with no recurring fees?

CapiCap and AI Subtitle Studio are $9.99 each, and Caption Plug is $14.99 if you already own Premiere Pro. Free options exist too: the WithSubtitles browser editor, and the Offline Captions web version for clips up to 5 minutes.

Is Caption Plug really a one-time payment?

Its pricing page says yes: $14.99 once for the Premiere Pro plugin, including all v1.x updates and 3 machines. It needs Premiere Pro, which Adobe sells separately, and its browser studio uses pay-as-you-go credits.

Do one-time caption apps work offline?

All six tools here transcribe on your own machine. Some send data out only for optional extras, like AI Subtitle Studio translation or Caption Plug cloud transcription keys.

What is a good alternative to CapCut Pro for auto captions without a subscription?

For animated short-form captions without an editor: Offline Captions (Windows) or WithSubtitles Desktop. Inside Premiere: Caption Plug. On iPhone: CapiCap. See Offline Captions vs CapCut.
