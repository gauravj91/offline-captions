Best auto caption apps for Reels, TikTok and Shorts (2026)

By Gaurav Jindal, founder · Updated 3 Nov 2026

Offline Captions is a pay-once caption app for creators: it adds animated word-by-word captions to videos on your own computer (Windows now, Mac coming) or free in your browser for clips up to 5 minutes.

Short answer: for the bold, animated word-by-word look with no editing, Submagic (from $12/month billed yearly) and Captions (Max $24.99/month) are the specialists. Instagram's free Edits app exports with no watermark if you only need clean captions on an iPhone. CapCut is the editor most creators already have, but its own pricing guide lists the auto caption generator as Pro ($19.99/month or $179.99/year). OpusClip suits cutting Shorts out of long videos, and VEED and Kapwing are browser editors with auto subtitles that watermark free exports. If you want to pay once and keep footage on your device: CapiCap ($9.99 lifetime, iPhone, iPad and Apple-silicon Mac), WithSubtitles ($49 desktop) or Offline Captions ($39 once, Windows; we make it).

How we picked

We started from the questions creators actually ask AI assistants ("best auto caption apps for Reels and TikTok", "which app adds word-by-word animated captions automatically", "auto caption apps for solo creators on a budget") and listed the apps those answers keep naming, plus the pay-once and on-device options they mostly miss. Then we opened each maker's own pricing and feature pages on 2 Nov 2026 and recorded only what they say: price, whether captions animate word by word, whether it works without uploading, what you can export and whether the free plan stamps a watermark. We did not run a head-to-head accuracy test between these apps, so there is no accuracy ranking here.

Who each is best for

  • Submagic: creators who want viral-style animated captions, zooms and B-roll from one upload, and are happy on a monthly plan with a per-video cap.
  • Captions: phone-first talking-head creators who also want AI editing and generation, on a $24.99/month plan.
  • CapCut: people who edit everything in CapCut anyway. Check the auto caption paywall: CapCut's Standard-vs-Pro guide says it is Pro-only, while its CapCut Web caption page calls the feature free.
  • Edits: Instagram-first creators on iPhone who want a free editor with captions and no watermark.
  • OpusClip: podcasters and streamers who need clips cut from long videos, with captions applied automatically.
  • VEED: teams and marketers who want a full browser editor with subtitles, translation and AI tools, on a per-seat plan.
  • Kapwing: browser editing with auto-subtitling and SRT/VTT downloads, if a watermark is fine on the free plan or you pay for Pro.
  • CapiCap: iPhone, iPad or Apple-silicon Mac users who want on-device captions and a one-off $9.99 unlock.
  • WithSubtitles: desktop users who want a free in-browser caption editor, with a $49 desktop app for batch and faster export.
  • Offline Captions: Windows users who post often and want word-by-word captions made offline, paid once. It has a Hinglish mode for Hindi-English speech in Roman script.

The apps, one by one

Submagic

Upload a video or YouTube link and get animated captions that "highlight, bounce, and fade word by word", per its caption page. The free tier gives 3 videos a month with a watermark; Starter is $19 a month or $12 billed yearly for 15 videos up to 2 minutes each. Exports go up to 4K/60 on Business.

Captions

A mobile and web app with 100+ caption styles, including animated word-by-word captions, and 100+ languages. Paid plans start at Max, $24.99 a month (iOS price), which bundles AI editing and generation credits. There is a free version; its watermark policy is not stated on the pricing page.

CapCut

A full editor on phone, desktop and web with customizable animated caption templates. CapCut's own comparison (dated 23 Sep 2026) puts Pro at $19.99 a month or $179.99 a year and lists the auto caption generator among Pro-only tools.

Edits

Instagram's free video editor. Its App Store listing says it generates captions automatically, offers caption style presets and exports up to 4K with no watermark. The listing we checked is iPhone-only.

OpusClip

A clipping tool first: it finds moments in long videos and adds animated captions with emoji and keyword highlights. Free gives 60 credits a month with a watermark and exports that expire after 3 days; Starter is $15 a month, Pro $29 a month or $14.50 billed yearly.

VEED

A browser video editor with auto subtitles, translation and AI generation. Per VEED help center, free and logged-out exports carry a VEED.IO watermark that only a paid plan removes, and free exports are 720p (Creator 1080p, Pro and up 4K). Its pricing page builds prices in the browser and we saw them vary, so check it in your region.

Kapwing

A browser editor with a Subtitler tool. Per its pricing page (3 Nov 2026), the free plan exports with a watermark at 720p, up to 4 minutes, with 10 minutes of auto-subtitling; Pro starts at $16 a month and removes the watermark. SRT and VTT downloads are listed.

CapiCap

On-device captions for iPhone, iPad and Apple-silicon Macs. It can light up each word as it is spoken, exports burned-in video or SRT, and keeps your video's resolution. The first 3 exports each month are free with no watermark; lifetime access is $9.99.

WithSubtitles

A free in-browser editor that downloads its speech model to your browser, so nothing is uploaded. The desktop app is $49 once, works offline and adds batch export, saved styles and SRT import. Its makers say transcription quality is the same in both.

Offline Captions (ours)

A Windows auto caption app (Mac version coming, no date yet) that makes word-by-word animated captions on your own computer and exports an MP4 with captions burned in plus an SRT. English scored 7.6% word error rate on our 28-clip benchmark. $39 once, or $29 at the founding price. Before buying, try the free browser version: clips up to 5 minutes, no signup. Skip it if you need a full editor, translation or a phone app.

FAQ

What are the best caption tools for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels in 2026?

For animated word-by-word captions, Submagic and Captions are the specialists on monthly plans. CapCut and Edits suit people already editing there; VEED and Kapwing are browser editors that watermark free exports. To pay once and caption on your own computer, CapiCap (Apple devices), WithSubtitles ($49) and Offline Captions ($39, Windows) are the options.

What is the best auto caption app for Reels and TikTok?

It depends on how you work. Submagic and Captions are the specialists for animated word-by-word captions on a monthly plan. CapCut suits people who already edit there. Edits is free with no watermark on iPhone. To pay once, look at CapiCap, WithSubtitles or Offline Captions.

Which app adds word-by-word animated captions automatically?

Submagic, Captions, CapiCap and Offline Captions all say they highlight or animate captions word by word. OpusClip adds animated captions with keyword highlights.

Is there a free auto caption app with no watermark?

Edits (Instagram, iPhone) exports with no watermark, CapiCap gives 3 free exports a month with no watermark, and the Offline Captions browser version is free for clips up to 5 minutes. Submagic, OpusClip, VEED and Kapwing watermark their free tiers.

Do auto caption apps upload my video?

Most do: Submagic, Captions, OpusClip, VEED and Kapwing process uploads in the cloud. CapiCap, WithSubtitles and Offline Captions transcribe on your own device.
