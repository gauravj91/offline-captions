FAQ

Questions

How do I add Hinglish captions to a video?

Open Offline Captions, choose Hinglish mode, drop in your video and export. It writes Hindi-English speech in Roman script, the way people type on WhatsApp (for example "Usko gaali de do, complaint kar lo"), times every word for the highlight animation, and burns the captions into an MP4. It also gives you an SRT file. Everything runs offline on your laptop.

Is there a Hinglish caption app without a monthly plan?

Yes. Offline Captions is a one-time purchase with no minute caps, because transcription runs on your own computer. The founding price for India is ₹1,499 once. For comparison, AIVideoCaption's Creator plan is ₹299 + GST for 30 days with 60 export minutes (checked Oct 2026).

Is CapCut available in India?

CapCut has been blocked in India since 2020, as reported by The Economic Times. Creators in India use other apps for auto captions; Offline Captions is a desktop option that runs on your laptop without uploading your videos.

How accurate are Hinglish captions?

Hinglish is harder than English. On 10 real code-mixed creator and podcast clips, about 22–24% of words differed from the reference transcript in our test, and part of that is spelling variation between Roman and Devanagari. Expect to fix a few words per clip. Words the app could not time precisely are flagged in yellow, and the personal dictionary remembers your corrections.

Does it support Hindi in Devanagari, Tamil, Marathi or other Indian languages?

Not yet. Hinglish in Roman script and English are available. Hindi in Devanagari and other Indian languages are coming, and we will list each one when it is ready.

What is Shorts Studio, and can I buy it now?

Not yet. Shorts Studio is a planned add-on that turns a long video or podcast into ready Shorts on your own computer: it cuts silences and retakes, shortlists 8–15 clips for you to pick from, crops to vertical around whoever is speaking, and keeps your captions in sync. The planned price is ₹2,499 once with captions included, with an upgrade price for Offline Captions owners. It isn't for sale and nothing ships today.
