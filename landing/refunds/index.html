Refund Policy

By Gaurav Jindal, founder · Updated 19 Oct 2026

Offline Captions is a pay-once caption app for creators: it adds animated word-by-word captions to videos on your own computer (Windows now, Mac coming) or free in your browser for clips up to 5 minutes.

If Offline Captions isn't right for you, get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. No questions asked.

How it works

  • Request a refund within 14 days of your purchase date for a full refund of the amount you paid.
  • To ask, email us at hello@offlinecaptions.com from your order email, or reply to your Dodo Payments receipt.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. Dodo Payments usually processes them within a few business days; your bank may take longer to show it.
  • When a refund is issued, the license key stops working.

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller and Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments (dodopayments.com), who also handles order-related inquiries and returns. Their buyer terms apply to the payment.
